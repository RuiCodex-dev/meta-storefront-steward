How to judge (one click per card; about 30 seconds each)

Read the review (the English gloss is under every non-English text). If both the category and the severity are right, press Agree. If either is wrong, choose the right category (or keep it if only the severity is wrong), then press Disagree. The tie-breaks: a crash wins over everything; a money problem is billing; praise with a wish is a feature request (S2); the star rating alone decides nothing.
